Privacy Policy

PixKilo: Photo KB Resizer

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PixKilo: Photo KB Resizer (“PixKilo”, “we”, “our” or “the app”) handles information when you use the app.

At a glance: Photo selection, resizing and compression are designed to take place on your device. PixKilo does not operate a service that uploads your selected photos to our servers.

1. Photos and files

When you choose a photo, PixKilo accesses the photo you select so that the app can resize or compress it. The processing is performed locally on your device. The app may save the resulting image to a location you choose or to the app’s designated picture folder, and may keep local compression-history information so you can view previous results.

PixKilo does not require you to create an account. We do not receive or store the photos you process on our own servers. Photos and locally stored history may remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app, subject to your device’s storage behavior and backup settings.

2. Advertising and third-party services

PixKilo may use Google AdMob to display banner and rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostics and device information to provide, measure, limit or personalize ads, subject to your device settings, applicable consent requirements and Google's policies.

For information about how Google uses data, visit Google’s advertising policy and Google’s Privacy Policy.

3. Permissions

The app requests only permissions or system access needed for its features, such as selecting an image and saving a compressed image. Android may provide access through the system photo picker without granting broad access to your photo library.

4. Data retention and deletion

Images and compression-history records handled by PixKilo are stored locally on your device. You can delete saved images using your device’s file or photo manager and remove history entries using the app’s history controls, if available. Uninstalling the app removes app-private data, but may not remove images you previously saved to shared device storage.

Advertising-related information is handled by Google and its partners under their own retention and deletion practices.

5. Children’s privacy

PixKilo is not designed specifically for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and age-related settings should be configured in accordance with applicable laws and Google Play requirements.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps in designing the app to keep its on-device processing and local data handling appropriate for its functionality. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Your choices

You can control photo selection and saving through Android, manage advertising settings through your device’s settings where available, and choose whether to interact with advertisements. Availability of controls may vary by device and region.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app’s features, services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this page with a revised effective date.
